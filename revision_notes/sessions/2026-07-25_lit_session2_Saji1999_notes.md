## What is already established (from the B.2 verification — do not re-litigate)

Carried in so the comprehension pass builds on it rather than repeating it:

- **1997 was a positive IOD** — listed as a strong-ENSO co-occurrence year, and one of the
  six composite events. CONFIRMED at source.
- **"Independent of ENSO" is SCOPED, not absolute.** Saji shows the DMI is a *distinct
  mode* (weak DMI–Niño3 correlation; pure non-ENSO cases in other years) — NOT that it
  never co-occurs. 1997 is a co-occurrence year sitting right next to the independence claim.
- **Independent of ENSO ≠ independent of everything** — the discussion ties the dipole to
  the monsoon circulation and notes a shared biennial tendency. Hold this; it's the
  subtlety most secondary summaries flatten.

## Comprehension pass — questions to answer FROM THE TEXT (not from memory, not from Claude)

*Fill each from the paper. Where a claim rests on a figure, name the figure. Where a number
appears, take it from the text/table, never eyeballed off a plot (fn-4 lesson).*

**Q1 — What is the DMI, precisely?**
How is the Dipole Mode Index constructed — which two boxes, which variable, which sign
convention? (The whole paper hangs on this definition; get it exactly.)

**Q2 — What makes it a "dipole" rather than just "Indian Ocean warming"?**
What is the spatial structure of a positive event — where are the SST anomalies, and what
are the *wind* and *thermocline/upwelling* anomalies that accompany them? What makes the
two poles a coupled see-saw rather than two unrelated patches?

**Q3 — The independence evidence, in the paper's own terms.**
What exactly does Saji offer as proof the DMI is distinct from ENSO? (Correlation value;
the pure non-ENSO cases and which years; why those years matter.) State the claim at the
strength the paper states it — no stronger.

**Q4 — The air–sea feedback.**
What is the positive feedback loop that lets the event grow? (Wind → thermocline → SST →
back to wind.) This is the IOD's analogue of Bjerknes feedback on the *zonal* Pacific —
note the parallel explicitly, and note where it breaks down / differs.

**Q5 — Seasonality and phase-locking.**
When does the IOD develop, peak, and decay through the year? Why is it phase-locked to that
season (link to the monsoon)? This is what the NOW note meant by "Oct phase-locking stacks
on SON/OND panels."

**Q6 — What the paper does NOT claim.**
Where does the discussion hedge? (Monsoon dependence; biennial tendency; anything about
predictability or forcing it explicitly leaves open.) Cataloguing the hedges is how you
avoid over-citing it later.
